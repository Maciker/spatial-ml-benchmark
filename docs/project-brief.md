# Project brief

## Question

How much does reported model performance change when observations are evaluated in locations separated from their training data?

## Deliverable

A public benchmark comparing a baseline and Random Forest under random and spatially grouped validation, with error maps and a short explanation of generalisation limits.

## Success criteria

- The task, labels, features, splits, and metrics are documented.
- Random and spatial results are reported side by side.
- The final conclusion names failure modes rather than presenting one accuracy figure as a product claim.
